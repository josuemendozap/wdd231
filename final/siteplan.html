Site Plan: Familia Sazón

A planning document for a 3-page website project about a family business that sells food products.

Site Name

Site Name: Familia Sazón

This name was selected because it represents a family business with homemade flavor and tradition. The word "Sazón" communicates taste, authenticity, and Mexican-style cooking.

Optional Domain Idea: familia-sazon.com

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to promote and grow a family food business by providing information about the company, displaying product offerings, and allowing customers to order products online.

The site will include:

  • A home page with company information and customer reviews.
  • A products page with a product gallery, modal dialogs with product details, and an order form.
  • An about/contact page with the family story, mission/values, and location.

Scenarios

These are questions a visitor might ask when using the website:

  • What products does Familia Sazón sell and how much do they cost?
  • Can I see nutritional information and recipes before ordering?
  • How can I place an order and how long will it take to receive it?
  • Where is the business located and how can I contact the family?

Color Scheme

The color scheme was selected to represent warmth, food, tradition, and trust.

Primary Color: #8B1E3F

Used for header background, headings, and main branding elements.

Accent Color: #F4A261

Used for section borders, badges, highlights, and call-to-action accents.

Background Color: #FFF7F0

Used as the page background for a soft and clean food-friendly look.

Typography

The fonts were chosen to be modern, clean, and friendly while still feeling professional.

Heading Font: Poppins

Used for headings (h1, h2, h3) because it is bold, modern, and highly readable.

Body Font: Nunito

Used for paragraphs, lists, and general text because it is friendly, clean, and easy to read.
