About Familia Sazón

Familia Sazón is a small family food business in Mexico City (CDMX). We started cooking for friends and neighbors, and now we offer our menu online.

Our mission

Offer delicious traditional food with honest prices, fast ordering, and friendly service.

Delivery

We deliver in CDMX zones: Centro, Norte, Sur, Oriente, and Poniente.

Hours

Friday and Saturday: 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM
Weekdays: pre-orders only
