NP Membership Benefits

Cost: Free

Audience: Non-profit organizations

  • Basic business directory listing
  • Access to community announcements
  • Invitations to select networking events
  • Support for local non-profit initiatives

Bronze Membership Benefits

Cost: Low tier (entry level)

  • Everything included in NP Membership
  • Enhanced directory listing
  • Access to member-only networking events
  • Basic training workshops
  • Eligibility for chamber promotions

Silver Membership Benefits

Cost: Mid tier

  • Everything included in Bronze Membership
  • Spotlight position on the home page (rotating)
  • Discounts on chamber-hosted events
  • Advanced business training sessions
  • Social media promotion opportunities

Gold Membership Benefits

Cost: Premium tier

  • Everything included in Silver Membership
  • Featured advertising in newsletters
  • Highest event discounts
  • Priority sponsorship opportunities
  • Exclusive invitations to leadership events
